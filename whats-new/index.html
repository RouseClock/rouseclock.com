What's new

What changed in each version of the Rouse firmware. Clocks offer new versions on their web page (Settings → Firmware); nothing installs until you tap Install.

Rouse 1.0.0

November 4, 2026

  • Rouse 1.0: a bedside alarm clock for three ESP32 3.5-inch display boards (Freenove FNK0104N, Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5 and the "CYD" E32R35T), set up and controlled from your phone's browser.
  • Up to 10 alarms with their own days, sound, volume and snooze; a gentle volume ramp, sunrise light and Extra loud; skip the next alarm for a holiday.
  • Snooze bar on top (Freenove and CYD), or tap the case to snooze (Waveshare).
  • Night mode with a dim red theme; weather, date and next alarm on the clock; your own themes, photos and daytime slideshows.
  • Sleep sounds with a sleep timer, an hourly chime and your own sounds.
  • Updates from the clock's web page with one tap, an optional web page PIN, and settings backup and restore.
  • New in this version: an About section in Settings, with links to the manual, What's new and a tip page; the update check also runs when Settings is opened.

Rouse 0.5.3-beta

November 4, 2026

  • When an update is available, the clock's web page now links to this What's new page.
  • Updates that need you to do something afterwards say so before you install them.

Rouse 0.5.2-beta

November 4, 2026

  • The clock's web page checks rouseclock.com for updates and offers to install them. Nothing installs until you say so.
  • Waveshare: tap the case to snooze, and tap twice quickly to stop the alarm or cancel a snooze. On by default; Settings → Hardware has the options.
  • The Settings page refreshes faster, and the clock's log has fewer false "slow" warnings.
  • The log holds 250 lines instead of 90 on the Freenove and Waveshare.
