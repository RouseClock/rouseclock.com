What's new

What changed in each version of the Rouse firmware. Clocks offer new versions on their web page (Settings → Firmware); nothing installs until you tap Install.

Rouse 2.0.1

November 26, 2026

  • Fixed: at power-up the clock sometimes opened its WiFi setup hotspot for a few seconds, even though your network was fine.
  • Fixed: a rare restart a few seconds after the radio started playing at power-up (Freenove and Waveshare).
  • Waveshare: the screen now comes on reliably at power-up on the bare board, as well as in Waveshare's enclosure.
  • The Status page now shows a "Last crash" only for the firmware that's running: after an update, an old one is cleared.

Rouse 2.0.0

November 24, 2026

Before you update: If you added the clock's page to your phone's home screen, remove the icon and add it again after updating, to get the new look.

  • Rouse 2.0 is the connected bedside station: clock, radio, photos and weather, for your nightstand or your desk. The top of the clock's menu chooses what the screen shows: Clock, Radio, Weather or Carousel.
  • Internet radio (Freenove and Waveshare): find stations on the Sounds tab by call letters, name, city or style, or add one by its address. Wake up to a station (with a backup sound if it doesn't answer), fall asleep to one, or just listen on the Radio view. Choose what's behind the Radio view: the clock's theme, a color, a gradient or a photo.
  • Weather view: now, the next hours and the next five days, with rain chances. The sky behind it follows the weather and warms up around sunrise and sunset.
  • Carousel: your photos, and if you like the clock and the weather, taking turns. Name and rename photos, and choose which ones it shows.
  • One simple pattern on the clock: tap the screen for the menu, or for the page of whatever view is showing. The views are always along the top, and Alarms & settings is one tap away.
  • Your clock on your phone: the Home tab shows a live picture of the clock, and its buttons work (the radio's, and Snooze and Stop). Turn on Play alarms here and your phone rings too while the page is open. Add the page to your home screen for an app-like icon.
  • Steadier WiFi: the clock sticks with your main network, moves back to it from a backup by itself, returns to the network that was working if a new one doesn't connect, and keeps its setup network easy for phones to find.
  • Better troubleshooting: the log now keeps the last lines of the previous few runs, through restarts and power cuts, and notes when WiFi is connected but the internet isn't answering.
  • CYD: fixed the faint static (a pair of scratches every couple of seconds) in sleep sounds, and alarm sounds are smoother. On the CYD, alarms now play at 25% volume or more and sleep sounds at 15% or more, where its simple speaker output still sounds clean.
  • Fixed: internet radio could stop on its own after a while (Freenove and Waveshare). The Freenove's web page could fail to load ("low memory").

Rouse 1.0.3

November 11, 2026

  • New: Extra loud for sleep sounds (Sounds → Sleep sounds), for a noisy room or a heavy sleeper.
  • Smoother sleep sounds: white noise, pink noise and rain have a softer top end, without the raspy edge on the clock's small speaker.
  • Fixed: if the weather server stopped answering partway through a reply, the clock could restart itself (shown as "task watchdog" under Last restart). Alarms and settings were never affected, but the screen went blank for a few seconds.
  • Settings → About Rouse now shows the support email address.

Rouse 1.0.0

November 4, 2026

  • Rouse 1.0: a bedside alarm clock for three ESP32 3.5-inch display boards (Freenove FNK0104N, Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5 and the "CYD" E32R35T), set up and controlled from your phone's browser.
  • Up to 10 alarms with their own days, sound, volume and snooze; a gentle volume ramp, sunrise light and Extra loud; skip the next alarm for a holiday.
  • Snooze bar on top (Freenove and CYD), or tap the case to snooze (Waveshare).
  • Night mode with a dim red theme; weather, date and next alarm on the clock; your own themes, photos and daytime slideshows.
  • Sleep sounds with a sleep timer, an hourly chime and your own sounds.
  • Updates from the clock's web page with one tap, an optional web page PIN, and settings backup and restore.
  • New in this version: an About section in Settings, with links to the manual, What's new and a tip page; the update check also runs when Settings is opened.

Rouse 0.5.3-beta

November 4, 2026

  • When an update is available, the clock's web page now links to this What's new page.
  • Updates that need you to do something afterwards say so before you install them.

Rouse 0.5.2-beta

November 4, 2026

  • The clock's web page checks rouseclock.com for updates and offers to install them. Nothing installs until you say so.
  • Waveshare: tap the case to snooze, and tap twice quickly to stop the alarm or cancel a snooze. On by default; Settings → Hardware has the options.
  • The Settings page refreshes faster, and the clock's log has fewer false "slow" warnings.
  • The log holds 250 lines instead of 90 on the Freenove and Waveshare.
